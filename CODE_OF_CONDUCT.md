# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or publication of other people's private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept that maintainers make the final call on what is merged.

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel used for the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to dariusz@honeylens.io. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block participants who do not follow this code of conduct.
